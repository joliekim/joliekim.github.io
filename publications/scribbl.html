Sketching With Haptics: Authoring Multimodal Soft-Haptic Cues Without Explicit Parameters

Heeji Jolie Kim, Heeju Mun, Ji-Sung Kim, Ki-Uk Kyung, Andrea Bianchi

PDF DOI (coming soon)

Abstract

Authoring haptic feedback has traditionally relied on specifying explicit parameters, with recent efforts shifting toward text-based generation. However, bodily expression is often preverbal, text alone is insufficient to capture its subtle nuances or to support rapid, exploratory articulation of as-yet-unnamed sensations. We present Scribbl, a tool for authoring multimodal haptics, built around sketching. A designer starts from a felt moment, sketches it as a pattern, refines it in words or by hand while feeling it on the body, and collects patterns that are then composed across body-worn actuators into a whole sequence, all without entering explicit parameters or wiring logic.

Keywords: haptic authoring, sketching, multimodal actuators, large language models, interaction design
